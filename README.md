# Dewly

**Personalized skincare education, grounded in evidence and designed around privacy.**

Dewly is an ambitious proposed AI skincare platform that combines facial photographs with self-reported skincare routines, lifestyle, and diet. It helps people understand supported visible skin features, explore appropriate skincare products and serums, track changes, and seek qualified professional advice.

This repository contains a software engineering homework deliverable: an **initial, AI-assisted requirements elicitation backlog**, not a working application or clinically validated medical product.

## Problem

People encounter conflicting skincare advice and spend time and money trying products without understanding suitability, risks, or realistic expectations. Dewly aims to make skincare information understandable, personalized, accessible, and safer while helping users recognize when professional care is appropriate.

## Unlimited-capital vision

In this hypothetical project, unlimited capital supports diverse clinical research, dermatologist and registered-dietitian teams, high-quality ingredient databases, secure infrastructure, accessibility, localization, and long-term validation. Funding enables ambitious research; it cannot guarantee perfect accuracy or eliminate uncertainty.

The long-term platform includes:
- Guided facial photography and supported observations with confidence and limitations.
- Personalized cosmetic skincare routines, including serum categories and compatible product options.
- Optional lifestyle and nutrition education based on user-provided information.
- Longitudinal progress tracking, user feedback, and professional review.
- Clinician-approved educational content and independently governed model updates.
- Affordable product alternatives and transparent commercial relationships.

## Proposed user journey

1. Create an account and review eligibility, consent, and privacy choices.
2. Share optional goals, current products, sensitivities, and relevant lifestyle context.
3. Capture photographs with lighting, focus, and framing guidance.
4. Receive supported observations or a clear explanation that the image cannot be assessed.
5. Explore a routine with rationale, cautions, realistic expectations, and professional referrals.
6. Record adherence, reactions, and comparable follow-up photographs.

## Safety boundaries

Photographs alone must not be used to assert a diagnosis, nutrient deficiency, allergy, or cause of a skin concern. Dewly must not promise perfect accuracy, guaranteed improvement, or causal conclusions from before-and-after photographs. Low-confidence and unsupported cases require abstention or professional referral.

The initial consumer product provides skincare education and cosmetic routine support. It does not independently prescribe medication, replace emergency care, or recommend restrictive diets or supplements as treatment. Individualized medical and nutrition care belongs with qualified professionals. Future diagnostic or treatment functionality requires a separate intended-use decision, clinical validation, and applicable regulatory review.

## Privacy commitments

Facial photographs and health-related context are sensitive. Proposed requirements include explicit consent, minimum necessary collection, encryption, access controls, retention limits, export, and deletion. Research and model training require separate, optional consent. Facial identification, selling sensitive information, and targeted advertising based on health or facial data are outside scope.

No real user photographs, patient records, credentials, or other sensitive records belong in this repository.

## Requirements deliverable

[REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) contains requirement IDs, types, priorities, dependencies, testable acceptance criteria, stakeholders, assumptions, release gates, and open elicitation questions.

The source concept is the student's proposed AI skincare platform with unlimited capital. The backlog was generated with an OpenAI language model and reviewed for scope, dependency consistency, and testability. No stakeholder interviews, implementation, clinical studies, or performance measurements are claimed. All numerical targets are provisional requirements for stakeholder validation.

## Scope and roadmap

- **Pilot:** Adults only in a jurisdiction selected after legal review; safe onboarding, image capture, constrained observations, basic routines, and professional escalation.
- **Expansion:** Progress tracking, optional nutrition support, clinician services, product breadth, accessibility, and localization.
- **Research:** Separately consented studies and independently validated model improvements.

Safety and privacy release gates apply regardless of available capital.
